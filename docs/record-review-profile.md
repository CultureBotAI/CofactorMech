# Cofactor review profile

Follow the shared output contract in [record-reviews.md](record-reviews.md).

Review chemical identity and speciation; cofactor function and context; precursor versus direct supply; actual target identifiers and immutable source commits; evidence supporting each relation; explicit uncertainty and research gaps; and append-only curation history. Check every linked source and separate source existence from claim support. A structural test pass cannot substitute for scientific assessment. Review all records changed by a seed batch, account for excluded candidates, and preserve source licences and provenance. Confirmed findings become issues and must be fixed and re-reviewed before requesting a merge.
